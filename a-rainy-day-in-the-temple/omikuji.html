手水 · Purify

Rinse at the water basin

Before approaching the kami, you wash at the temizuya in a fixed order. Tap the basin to pour.

Water basin手水舎
  1. 01Left hand. Hold the ladle in your right hand and pour water over your left.
  2. 02Right hand. Switch hands and pour over your right.
  3. 03Mouth. Pour a little into your left palm and rinse your mouth. The ladle never touches your lips.
  4. 04Ladle. Tilt it upright so the last water runs down the handle, then set it back.

二礼二拍手一礼 · Pray

Two bows, two claps, one bow

Drop a coin into the offering box and ring the bell. Then greet the kami. After the claps, keep your hands together and make your wish silently.
