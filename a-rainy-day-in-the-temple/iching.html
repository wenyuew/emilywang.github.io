02 — Casting · 起卦

Hold the question, then toss

Press and hold the table to shake the coins in your hands, and let go to toss. On a phone you can shake the phone. A plain back counts three and an inscribed face counts two; six or nine is a moving line.

03 — The hexagram · 卦象

The primary hexagram is where things stand now. The nuclear hexagram, drawn from lines two to five, shows what is hidden inside. The changed hexagram, after the moving lines turn, is where it is heading.
