Before you enter

Bring one question

At the temple you tell the deity who you are and what you want to know. Keep it to one question, and make it specific.

上香 · Offer incense

Light three sticks

Press and hold the incense to light it. Then hold it at your forehead, bow three times, and tell the deity silently who you are and what you're asking.

Incense burner香炉

摇签 · Shake the cylinder

Shake until one stick falls

Kneel, tilt the bamboo cylinder forward and keep shaking. The sticks rise slowly until one drops out. If two fall together, they go back in. On a phone, shake the phone.

掷筊 · Ask for confirmation

Throw the moon blocks

Hold the stick and ask "Is this the one for me?" Then press and hold the blocks and let them fall. One flat side up and one round side up means yes.

Moon blocks筊杯
聖筊One flat, one round. Yes.
笑筊Both flat. The gods laugh. Throw again.
陰筊Both round. No. Shake again.

Other matters

Traditionally you ask once per question. If the answer is unclear, a temple interpreter (解签) reads the poem against your situation. These 20 slips were written for this app in the style of temple poems.
